❓ Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

This app helps you check for registered sexual offenders in your ZIP code area. It's designed for educational purposes and to help you stay aware of your community's safety.

Currently, this app uses sample data for demonstration purposes. In a real implementation, it would connect to official government databases. Always verify information through official sources like the National Sex Offender Public Website (NSOPW).

If you have immediate safety concerns, contact local law enforcement. For general information about registered offenders, contact your local police department or visit the official NSOPW website at www.nsopw.gov.

See our safety tips below, trust your instincts, stay aware of your surroundings, and don't hesitate to reach out to trusted adults or authorities if you feel unsafe.

💡 Stay Safe Out There!

👥
Stay With Groups

Travel with friends or family when possible, especially in unfamiliar areas or at night.

👀
Stay Aware

Keep your head up, avoid distractions like headphones, and trust your instincts about situations.

📱
Emergency Contacts

Always have emergency contacts readily available and know how to contact local authorities.

🏠
Know Your Area

Familiarize yourself with safe places, well-lit areas, and routes to and from your destinations.

💬
Communicate

Let trusted adults know where you're going and when you expect to return.

🛡️
Trust Your Instincts

If something doesn't feel right, remove yourself from the situation and seek help immediately.
